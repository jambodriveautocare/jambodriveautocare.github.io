Run by an Engineer

Our workshop is built on engineering principles, systematic fault diagnosis, and precision-driven automotive repair. We go beyond replacing faulty components by identifying the underlying causes of vehicle problems through Root Cause Analysis (RCA), cause-and-effect evaluation, and evidence-based troubleshooting.

Fuel Injection & Engine Management

We diagnose fuel delivery and engine performance problems by analysing fuel pressure, injector operation, air–fuel mixture, sensor signals, ignition performance, and ECU data. Using scan tools, live data analysis, and targeted testing, we identify whether a fault originates from the fuel system, electrical circuits, sensors, actuators, or mechanical engine components.

Transmission & Gearbox Diagnostics

We investigate transmission faults by evaluating fluid condition and level, hydraulic pressure, solenoid operation, sensor inputs, clutch engagement, and electronic control systems. For automatic and CVT transmissions, we use diagnostic data and systematic testing to distinguish between electrical, hydraulic, mechanical, and control-related faults before recommending repairs.

Drivetrain & Power Transmission

We trace drivetrain vibrations, knocking sounds, excessive play, power loss, and abnormal noise to their actual sources. Our inspection covers drive shafts, CV joints, differentials, propeller shafts, wheel bearings, engine and gearbox mounts, and related components. By assessing operating conditions, component wear, alignment, and load-related symptoms, we narrow down the cause of each fault.

Root Cause Analysis: Cause, Effect & Corrective Action

Our diagnostic process follows a structured engineering approach:

  1. Identify the symptom: Establish the customer's complaint and reproduce the fault under controlled conditions.
  2. Collect evidence: Review fault codes, live data, service history, physical inspection findings, and operating conditions.
  3. Analyse cause and effect: Apply techniques such as the 5 Whys, cause-and-effect (Ishikawa/Fishbone) diagrams, and fault-tree analysis to investigate possible failure mechanisms.
  4. Verify the root cause: Conduct targeted tests and compare results against manufacturer specifications before confirming the diagnosis.
  5. Implement corrective action: Repair or replace only the components justified by the findings, addressing contributing factors where necessary.
  6. Validate the repair: Clear relevant faults, perform functional tests or a road test where appropriate, and confirm that the original symptoms have been resolved.

Our engineering philosophy is simple: diagnose accurately, repair intelligently, and verify every result. This approach helps reduce unnecessary parts replacement, minimise repeat failures, control repair costs, and improve vehicle reliability, safety, and performance.
